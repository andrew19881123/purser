# Code of Conduct

This project adopts the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org),
version 2.1, as its Code of Conduct.

In short: we are committed to a harassment-free, welcoming and respectful
experience for everyone, regardless of background or identity. Be kind, assume
good faith, and focus on what is best for the community.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be
reported to the project maintainers at **andrew19881123@gmail.com**. All
complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. Maintainers are
obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter.

The full text of the Contributor Covenant v2.1 is available at
<https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/>.
